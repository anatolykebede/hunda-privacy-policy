Privacy Policy

Hunda Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026
App: Hunda (Android package: com.hunda.mobile)
Contact: support@hunda-inventory.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Hunda (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Hunda mobile application and related services for inventory, sales, customers, credits, banks, and business reporting.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to business users of Hunda, including company owners, administrators, and staff accounts created for a company workspace. Hunda is a business productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Hunda, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password/authentication credentials, role, and company affiliation.
  • Business data you enter: products, inventory, warehouses, shops, customers, suppliers, sales, invoices, credits, expenses, banks, and related notes or attachments.
  • Payment and subscription details: plan status and billing-related information needed to manage your subscription (payment card data is typically processed by payment providers, not stored in full by us).
  • Device and usage data: app version, device type/OS, IP address, crash/diagnostic logs, and basic usage events needed to operate and improve the service.
  • Locally cached data: selected business records may be stored on your device for offline/sync performance.
  • SMS-related data (Android, only if you grant permission): used to optionally scan bank/payment notification SMS (for example CBE, Telebirr, and similar messages) so the app can help record expenses or bank transactions. Only messages relevant to that feature are processed. We do not use SMS data for advertising or sell SMS content.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and sync inventory, sales, credit, banking, and reporting features
  • Authenticate users and manage company workspaces and roles
  • Process subscriptions and customer support requests
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues
  • Improve product reliability and performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with:

  • Other users in your company workspace, according to roles and permissions set by your company administrator
  • Service providers that help us host servers, send emails, process payments, or monitor reliability, under contractual confidentiality obligations
  • Legal authorities when required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security
  • Business transfers in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice where required

5. Data storage, security, and retention

  • Data is transmitted using modern encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) where supported.
  • We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect account and business data.
  • Business and account data are retained while your account/company workspace remains active and as needed for legal, accounting, dispute, or security purposes.
  • Cached data on a device can be removed by logging out, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.

6. Permissions (Android)

  • Internet: required to sign in and sync data with Hunda servers.
  • SMS (READ/RECEIVE), if granted: used only for optional/core-supported payment or notification matching features you enable. You can deny or revoke this permission in system settings; core inventory features that do not depend on SMS remain available.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access or update profile and company business records in the app (subject to your role)
  • Request account deletion or data deletion by contacting support@hunda-inventory.com
  • Revoke device permissions in Android settings
  • Stop using the service and uninstall the app at any time

When you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize associated personal data that is no longer needed, except where retention is required by law or legitimate business records retention (for example completed invoices your company must keep).

8. International processing

Hunda may process data on servers or with providers located in countries different from where you live. Where we do so, we take steps designed to protect information in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

9. Children’s privacy

Hunda is intended for business use and not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the effective date above and, when changes are material, provide additional notice in the app or by email where appropriate.
